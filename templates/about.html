What We Stand For

Our Core Values

  • 01 Holiness and Purity Members are expected to live a holy life, avoid sin, and maintain spiritual and physical cleanliness before God as white garments symbolizes purity
  • 02 Faith in Jesus Christ:-complete teachings and belief in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior, the source of salvation, healing, and deliverance.
  • 03 Absolute Obedience to the Holy Spirit:- The church believes its doctrine, worship pattern, and revelations are directed by the Holy Spirit, and members must be spiritually sensitive and obedient
  • 04 Prayer and Divine Worship:- Constant prayer, supplication, praise, fasting, and reverence in worship are central to spiritual growth.
  • 05 Evangelism:- Winning souls for Christ and spreading the gospel worldwide is a major assignment of the church.
  • 06 Discipline and Orderliness:- Members must respect church hierarchy, obey church rules, and maintain orderly conduct in worship and daily life.
  • 07 Love and Unity:- Promotion of brotherly love, peace, humility, forgiveness, and unity among members as one body in Christ.
  • 08 Separation from Idolatry and Worldliness:- Members are forbidden from fetish practices, charms, occultism, idol worship, and immoral worldly practices.
  • 09 Service to God and Humanity:- Every member is expected to serve faithfully through church duties, helping others, and supporting God's work.
  • 10Reverence for Divine Authority:- Respect for God's authority, church leadership and divine instructions is strongly emphasized.
Our Traditions

Church Tenets

  • • Purity & Sanctification: Members must undergo rites of sanctification, which include cleansing via water sprinkling and immersion baptism, to break away from their old lives. Menstruating women or anyone with bodily fluids (such as an open wound) must undergo purification before returning to the church.
  • • Dietary Restrictions: The consumption of pork, food sacrificed to idols, and any crawling animals is forbidden.
  • •Prohibition of Intoxicants: Members are strictly forbidden to consume alcohol, wine, or any strong drink that can intoxicate.
  • • No Smoking or Drugs: The smoking, snuffing, or inhaling of cigarettes, tobacco, or any type of weed is strictly prohibited, as the odor is believed to be repugnant to the Holy Spirit.
  • • Dress and Appearance: Members wear a white garment known as the Sutana. Wearing black or red apparel is forbidden (except for professional reasons). Head coverings are mandatory for female members.
  • • Sanctuary Protocol: Shoes must be removed before entering the church premises. Furthermore, male and female members must sit on opposite sides (gender segregation) during services.
  • • Rejection of the Occult: Participation in idolatry, black magic, charms, or any secret cults is absolutely forbidden.
  • • Worship Practices: Services rely heavily on prayer, chanting Halleluyah to the four corners of the earth, burning altar lights, and using incense, holy water, and perfume
