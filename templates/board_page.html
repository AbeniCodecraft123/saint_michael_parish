Governance

The Church Board

The highest policy-making, advisory, and oversight body of the parish — entrusted to preserve the vision, protect the doctrine, and guide Saint Michael Parish in strategic leadership and accountable governance.

Category One

Life Board Members

Appointed by the Founder, Life Board Members remain in office until the expiration of their tenure, resignation, removal under the constitution, or death.

Every Member, Same Standard

Standing to Serve

Qualifications

  • A committed and active church member, of good moral and spiritual character
  • Demonstrates leadership ability, integrity, and accountability
  • Has no unresolved disciplinary matter
  • Supports the vision and doctrine of the church, and is willing to serve selflessly

Code of Conduct

  • Demonstrate honesty, integrity, and respect for collective decisions
  • Avoid conflicts of interest and refrain from using office for personal gain
  • Maintain confidentiality and promote unity and peace
  • Remain accountable for their actions before God and the church
