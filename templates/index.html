Who we are

Rooted in faith, guided by light

Celestial Church of Christ, Saint Michael Parish, is a community of believers walking in the Aladura tradition — grounded in prayer, revelation, and the pursuit of holiness. We gather to worship in spirit and truth, guided by the Foundational Blueprint and Constitution of the Church.
